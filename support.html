Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my data?
You can reset all data in your Profile. Go to the Profile tab and tap "Reset All Data" to start fresh. This will reset your streak, tree progress, and all settings.
How do I add friends?
First, make sure you're signed in to your account. Then go to the Friends tab and enter your friend's unique code to connect. You can find your own code in your Profile to share with friends.
What happens if I go over my app limit?
If you exceed your daily limit for a blocked app, your tree resets and you can start fresh the next day. This helps maintain accountability and encourages you to stay within your goals.
Why is the continue button not working?
If the continue button isn't responding, you may need to enable certain permissions. Check your device settings to ensure:
  • Do Not Disturb is turned off
  • Screen Time access is enabled for Little Trees
  • Notification access is enabled for Little Trees
You can adjust these permissions in your device's Settings app under Screen Time and Notifications.
How do I enable Screen Time permissions?
  1. Open your device's Settings app
  2. Go to Screen Time
  3. Ensure Screen Time is enabled
  4. Grant Little Trees access when prompted
Is my data private?
Yes! Your privacy is our priority. All app usage data is stored locally on your device. We never track which specific apps you block or when you use them. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Can I use Little Trees without an account?
Absolutely! The core features of Little Trees work entirely offline without creating an account. An account is only needed if you want to use the Friends feature to connect with others.
